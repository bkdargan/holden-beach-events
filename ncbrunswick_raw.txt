Brunswick County Events
Local Flair & Flavor
A
Brunswick Islands vacation
experience goes beyond the beach. It also includes a
variety of festivals
and events for families, foodies and fun-lovers that will introduce you to local favors and customs of the area.
The
NC 4th of July Festival
has been running continuously for more than 200 years and is an annual favorite, as are the
NC Festival by the Sea
and
NC Oyster Festival
. Free outdoor summer concerts, movies and farmers' markets are some other the local events sure to entertain the whole family.
Use the calendar below to explore our events list, and then, have fun as you enjoy the best of the Brunswick Islands. You can adjust the dates to match your travel schedule and/or sort by event category in the sidebar on the left.
